8 games, scored the standard way

Pick a game, add your players, and play. Card Night knows each game's scoring, and you can switch common house rules on or off.

Canasta cousin with a hand, a foot and big book bonuses.

Canasta

Spades

Gin Rummy

500 Rum

Score what you meld, lose what you hold. First past 500.

Mexican Train

Dominoes, 13 rounds from double 12 down. Lowest total wins.

Cribbage

Peg to 121 with a tap board and a hand counter that knows every fifteen.

Euchre

Five tricks, two teams, first to 10. Marches and loners built in.

What you get

  • 8 classic gamesHand and Foot, Canasta, Spades, Gin Rummy, 500 Rum, Mexican Train, Cribbage and Euchre, scored the standard published way.
  • Up to 8 playersIn Hand and Foot, 500 Rum and Mexican Train. Teams or individual play where each game allows it.
  • House rulesSwitch common variations on or off. The defaults follow the standard published rules.
  • Fix any mistakeTap any past round to fix it (in Cribbage, undo the last peg).
  • Readable dark modeBig, clear numbers and a dark mode that stays easy to read in a dim room.
  • Keeps the screen awakeDuring a game, on devices that allow it.
  • Share the resultSend the final scoreboard as a picture with one tap.
  • Your games, your deviceNo ads. No tracking. Sign in only to sync. Finished games are saved on your device, with a backup file you control.

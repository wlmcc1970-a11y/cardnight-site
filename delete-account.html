Delete your account

Effective October 2, 2026 (version 1.1.0).

You only have an account if you chose to sign in. Card Night works fully without one. If you did sign in, the quickest way to delete it: open Card Night, tap Synced at the top of the home screen, then Delete my account. It takes a few seconds.

No longer have the app?

Email will@digirunestudios.com from the email address you signed in with (your Apple or Google account), with the subject "Delete my Card Night account". We will delete it within 30 days and write back to confirm. You do not need to reinstall the app.

What is deleted

  • Your Card Night account and sign-in record.
  • The online copy of your games: games in progress, finished games, player names you have used, and the list of games you deleted.

What is kept

A small record holding only your random account ID and the time of deletion, so your other signed-in devices cannot copy your games back to the cloud. Games already on a phone or tablet stay on that device until you delete them in the app or uninstall Card Night. Any purchase record the Apple App Store or Google Play keep under their own rules.

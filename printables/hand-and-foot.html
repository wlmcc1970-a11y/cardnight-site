How to score Hand and Foot

  • Card values: joker 50, 2 and ace 20, 8 to king 10, 4 to 7 5, black 3 5.
  • Melded cards count plus. Cards left in your hand or foot count minus.
  • Count every melded card, including the cards in your books, then add the book bonus.
  • Books of 7: clean (no wild cards) 500, dirty 300, all-wild 1500 if your table plays them.
  • Red 3s: plus 100 each laid down, minus 100 each caught in a hand or foot.
  • Going out adds 100.
  • First meld each round must reach 50, 90, 120, then 150. Bonuses do not count toward it.
  • Highest total after round 4 wins.

House rules vary from table to table. These are the standard published rules that Card Night uses by default.

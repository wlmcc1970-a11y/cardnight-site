How to score Cribbage

  • First to 121 wins, the moment they reach it, even in the middle of a hand.
  • If the starter card is a jack, the dealer pegs 2 (his heels).
  • Pegging: 15 scores 2, exactly 31 scores 2, a pair 2, three of a kind 6, four of a kind 12, a run 1 per card, the last card under 31 scores 1.
  • The show: count the non-⁠dealer’s hand, then the dealer’s hand, then the crib, each with the starter.
  • Hands: 2 for each 15, 2 for each pair, 1 per card in each run, flush 4 (5 with the starter), jack of the starter’s suit 1 (his nobs).
  • A crib flush counts only when all four crib cards and the starter share a suit (5).
  • Skunk: the loser has under 91. Double skunk: under 61.

House rules vary from table to table. These are the standard published rules that Card Night uses by default.

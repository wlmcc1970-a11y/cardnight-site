How to score 500 Rum

  • Card values: ace 15, but an ace played low in A-⁠2-⁠3 counts 1. Tens and face cards 10. Twos to nines count their number.
  • When someone goes out (or the stock runs out), add the cards you melded or laid off and subtract the cards left in your hand.
  • Your hand score and your total can both go below zero.
  • First to 500 or more wins. If two players pass 500 on the same hand, the higher score wins. A tie plays another hand.

House rules vary from table to table. These are the standard published rules that Card Night uses by default.

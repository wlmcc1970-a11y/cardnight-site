How to score Gin Rummy

  • Deadwood (unmatched cards): ace 1, number cards their number, jack, queen and king 10.
  • Knock with 10 or less deadwood. If yours is lower after the opponent lays off, you score the difference.
  • Undercut: if the opponent ties or beats the knocker, they score the difference plus 25.
  • Gin (no deadwood): 25 plus the opponent’s deadwood. Gin can never be undercut.
  • The game ends when a player reaches 100. That player adds 100, or 200 if the other player never scored.
  • Box bonus: each player adds 25 for every hand they won. The higher final score wins.
  • Classic values some tables use: gin 20, undercut 10, box 20.

House rules vary from table to table. These are the standard published rules that Card Night uses by default.

How to score Modern American Canasta

  • Card values: joker 50, 2 and ace 20, 8 to king 10, 4 to 7 5. Threes have no card value.
  • Count: melded cards plus, cards left in hand minus. With no canasta, melded cards count minus too.
  • Canastas: natural 500, mixed 300, aces or sevens 2500, all 2s 3000, four jokers and three 2s 2500, other wild 2000.
  • Unfinished melds: aces 2500, sevens 2500, wild cards 2000, taken off the base.
  • A player holding 3 or more aces, or 3 or more sevens, loses 1500 each, plus the cards.
  • Threes by color: 1 = 100, 2 = 300, 3 = 500, 4 = 1000. No canasta minus, one canasta zero, two or more plus.
  • Going out needs 2 canastas and a discard: 100. A 3 kept in hand: minus 100 (last stock card: minus 5).
  • First meld by total score: below 3000 needs 125, 3000 to 4995 needs 155, 5000 and up needs 180.
  • A special hand scores only its value for that side. Game: 8500.

House rules vary from table to table. These are the standard published rules that Card Night uses by default.

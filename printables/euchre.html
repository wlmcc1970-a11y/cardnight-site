How to score Euchre

  • The makers (the team that named trump) need at least 3 of the 5 tricks.
  • Makers take 3 or 4 tricks: 1 point. All 5 (a march): 2 points.
  • A player going alone who takes all 5 tricks: 4 points. Alone with 3 or 4 tricks: 1 point.
  • Makers take fewer than 3: they are euchred, and the other team scores 2.
  • First team to 10 wins.

House rules vary from table to table. These are the standard published rules that Card Night uses by default.

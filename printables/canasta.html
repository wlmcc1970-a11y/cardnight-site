How to score Canasta

  • Card values: joker 50, 2 and ace 20, 8 to king 10, 4 to 7 and black 3 5.
  • Cards: melded cards plus, cards left in hand minus. Count the cards inside canastas too.
  • Canastas: natural (no wild cards) 500, mixed 300.
  • Red 3s: 100 each, 800 for all four. If your side never melded, they count minus instead.
  • Going out 100. Going out concealed 200 in all.
  • First meld minimum by total score: below 0 needs 15, 0 to 1495 needs 50, 1500 to 2995 needs 90, 3000 and up needs 120.
  • Game: 5000 for 2 players or 2 teams, 7500 for 3 players.

House rules vary from table to table. These are the standard published rules that Card Night uses by default.

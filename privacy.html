Card Night: Score Keeper
Privacy Policy

Effective September 29, 2026. Last updated October 2, 2026 (version 1.1.0).

The short version: No ads and no tracking. You never need an account. If you do nothing, your games stay on your device. Only if you choose to sign in do we keep your sign-⁠in email and a copy of your Card Night games online, so they follow you to your other phones and tablets. We never sell or share it, and you can delete it any time.

Who we are

Card Night: Score Keeper ("Card Night") is made by DigiRune Studios (McCullough Development, Inc.) in Beaufort, South Carolina. Questions about this policy? Email will@digirunestudios.com.

What we collect

If you do not sign in: nothing. Card Night has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and we receive no information about you.

If you choose to sign in with Google or Apple (optional, in the Sign in button on the home screen), we keep:

  • Your account ID, and the email address and name Google or Apple shares with us (with Google, also a link to your profile picture; Apple lets you hide your real email and name). We use these only to sign you in and keep your games together.
  • A copy of your Card Night games and player names (described below), so they appear on each phone or tablet where you sign in.

We do not sell, rent or share this information, and we do not use it for advertising.

What stays on your device

To keep your scores, Card Night saves these things in your device's own app storage:

  • The game in progress: the game you picked, the house rules, player and team names you type, colors and each round's scores.
  • Your finished games in Saved games.
  • Your settings: light, dark or automatic display, larger text, and whether to keep the screen awake during a game.

If you are not signed in, this information never leaves your device. If you sign in, your games in progress, finished games, player names you have used and a list of games you deleted (so other devices delete them too) are also kept in your private cloud copy. Your settings are included in that copy, but each device always keeps its own display settings.

Where your cloud copy is kept

Sign-⁠in and the cloud copy use Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore, in the United States), operated by Google under its privacy terms. Only your own signed-⁠in account can read or change your copy. It is stored encrypted by Google.

Backups and shared pictures

If you choose Save backup, Card Night makes a file of your finished games on your device, and you decide where it goes (for example your files, a drive you use, or a message to yourself). If you tap Share the result, Card Night draws a picture of the final scores on your device and hands it to your phone's share menu, saves it to your downloads, or shows it on screen for you to save. A backup can also be shown as text for you to copy. We never receive any of these.

Printable score sheets

Print a blank score sheet opens or saves a PDF from this site. After Card Night is installed the sheets are kept on your device, so they also work offline. Nothing about you is sent.

Keeping the screen awake

While a game is open, Card Night can ask your device to keep the screen on. This uses a setting on your device only and sends nothing anywhere. You can turn it off in Settings and help.

Internet use

Card Night works offline. It goes online to download its own app files (including the printable score sheets) when you install or update it, and, only if you have signed in, to keep your cloud copy up to date. The servers that host the app files (Google Firebase Hosting and GitHub Pages) receive the ordinary technical details any website request includes, such as your IP address, under their own privacy statements. We do not use that information. The "More from DigiRune Studios" link opens our website only when you tap it.

Purchases and ratings

If you bought Card Night from the Apple App Store or Google Play, the purchase is handled by Apple or Google under their privacy policies. We do not receive your payment details. If you choose to rate the app, the store handles the rating.

Deleting your data

  • Delete one finished game: open Saved games, tap the game, then tap Delete from history.
  • Delete everything: uninstall Card Night, or clear its storage in your device settings.
  • Delete a game in progress: tap Delete on it on the home screen.
  • Delete your account and your cloud copy: tap Synced on the home screen, then Delete my account. This removes your account and the online copy right away. The games on your device stay there.
  • Or email will@digirunestudios.com from your sign-⁠in email and we will delete your account and cloud copy for you.
  • Full steps, including how to ask without the app: Delete your Card Night account.

If you never signed in, there is no account and no copy on any server.

Children

Card Night does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Signing in is optional and is not needed to use any feature of the app.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, we will update this page and the date at the top. If Card Night ever starts to handle any other personal information, this page will say so before that version is released.

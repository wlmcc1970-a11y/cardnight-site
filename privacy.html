Card Night: Score Keeper
Privacy Policy

Effective September 29, 2026. Last updated September 29, 2026 (version 1.0.2).

The short version: No ads, no tracking, no account. Your games stay on your device. Card Night does not collect, store or share any information about you.

Who we are

Card Night: Score Keeper ("Card Night") is made by DigiRune Studios (McCullough Development, Inc.) in Beaufort, South Carolina. Questions about this policy? Email will@digirunestudios.com.

What we collect

Nothing. Card Night has no sign-in, no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party code that collects data. We do not sell or share information, because we never receive any.

What stays on your device

To keep your scores, Card Night saves these things in your device's own app storage:

  • The game in progress: the game you picked, the house rules, player and team names you type, colors and each round's scores.
  • Your finished games in Saved games.
  • Your settings: light, dark or automatic display, and whether to keep the screen awake during a game.

This information never leaves your device. We cannot see it, and nobody else can through us.

Backups and shared pictures

If you choose Save backup, Card Night makes a file of your finished games on your device, and you decide where it goes (for example your files, a drive you use, or a message to yourself). If you tap Share the result, Card Night draws a picture of the final scores on your device and hands it to your phone's share menu, saves it to your downloads, or shows it on screen for you to save. A backup can also be shown as text for you to copy. We never receive any of these.

Keeping the screen awake

While a game is open, Card Night can ask your device to keep the screen on. This uses a setting on your device only and sends nothing anywhere. You can turn it off in Settings and help.

Internet use

Card Night works offline. The only times it goes online are to download its own app files when you install or update it. The server that hosts those files (GitHub Pages) receives the ordinary technical details any website request includes, such as your IP address, under GitHub's own privacy statement. We do not receive or use that information. The "More from DigiRune Studios" link opens our website only when you tap it.

Purchases and ratings

If you bought Card Night from the Apple App Store or Google Play, the purchase is handled by Apple or Google under their privacy policies. We do not receive your payment details. If you choose to rate the app, the store handles the rating.

Deleting your data

  • Delete one finished game: open Saved games, tap the game, then tap Delete from history.
  • Delete everything: uninstall Card Night, or clear its storage in your device settings.

There is no account to delete and no copy on any server.

Children

Card Night collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, we will update this page and the date at the top. If Card Night ever starts to handle any personal information, this page will say so before that version is released.
